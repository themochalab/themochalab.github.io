Department of Computer Science · Furman University

How people see, trust, and reason with data, and what changes when AI is in the loop.

MOCHA is a new research lab at Furman University. We study visualization literacy, perception, and trust; build human-AI and multi-agent systems; and bring what we learn to the people who rely on data every day, including practitioners, Blind and Low Vision (BLV) communities, clinicians, and scientists.

What MOCHA stands for

MMulti-agent
How AI agents, models, and people coordinate and reason together over visual data.
OObservational
Eye-tracking and behavioral studies of how people actually look at and scan visualizations.
CCognitive
The perceptual and cognitive mechanisms behind reading, and misreading, charts.
HHuman-AI
Trust and collaboration between people and AI/LLM systems in visual analytics.
AAesthetics
How style and design choices shape comprehension, engagement, and trust.

01Research

Six threads, one question.

How do people and AI systems make sense of visual data, and when should we trust what they conclude? Each thread links to the papers behind it.

  1. 01

    Cognition & perception

    How the mind processes, remembers, and sometimes misreads visual data, studied with eye-tracking and behavioral experiments.

    Related papers on cognition and perception

  2. 02

    Visualization & AI practitioners

    Benchmarks, literacy tests, and design guidelines for the people and AI systems that build and read charts.

    Related papers on visualization and AI practitioners

  3. 03 New

    Detecting AI-generated images

    When can we tell real images from generated ones, from everyday photos to chest radiographs, and how often do detectors raise false alarms?

    Related papers on detecting AI-generated images

  4. 04

    BLV & accessibility

    Extending visualization literacy and perception research to blind and low-vision readers.

    Early stage. Help shape it

  5. 05

    Medical & genomic visualization

    Visual tools for clinicians and researchers working with complex biomedical data.

    Related papers on medical and genomic visualization

  6. 06

    Environment & data science

    Visualization and interpolation methods for environmental and public-health data, like air quality at public schools.

    Related papers on environment and data science
